## 7c. Daily videos (Reels / Threads)

Nothing in it needs Claude: secrets are the existing `GEMINI_API_KEY`, `FB_PAGE_TOKEN`, `INSTAGRAM_TOKEN`, `THREADS_TOKEN`; only the steps that use a secret get it.
- Content (`src/lib/video/price-video.ts`, `tests/video.test.ts`), "ҮНЭ ТААГААРАЙ", built for young viewers on what short-video guidance agrees on (hook on screen in the first 2–3 s because many watch muted, a curiosity gap, a payoff every few seconds, a relatable comparison, an ending with a question; Instagram ranks watch time and sends): a guess "15 жилийн өмнө 1 кг үхрийн мах хэд байсан бэ?" with three option cards and a 3-2-1 (`PAUSE_S` silence inserted into the take after the question), the reveal (the true card turns green, confetti, the two `quizOptions` decoys struck: round numbers ×0.55 / ×1.8, labelled as options, never data; the true card's position moves with good + month), "Харин одоо?" with a counter running then → now over the chart and the "×6.6" chip, "Энэ мөнгөөр тэр үед 6 гаруй кг" as unit blocks (`units` = now / then, only when ≥ 1.5, else the % change), the 1-year sticker, "Танайд одоо хэд байна?". One video per good and NSO month (`videos.key` = `prices:<good>:<yyyy-mm>`): after 13 the runs make nothing until NSO publishes a month. `verifySpec` refuses digits in the narration, any caption number the snapshot does not hold, a quiz without exactly one true answer, a unit count ≠ now / then; posts (`posts.ts`) repeat the same numbers from the stored `VideoMeta`.
- "ХҮН БҮРТ ХУВААВАЛ" (`money-video.ts`, `money-html.ts`, Oct 2026) is OFF since Nov 6 2026 (the owner: a story's amount divided by the population reads as what each person pays, and what one citizen's taxes pay for cannot be told honestly from a news story, which never says which money is taxes, mining revenue or loans). - "30 СЕК МЭДЭЭ" (`impact-video.ts`, `impact-html.ts`, `tests/impact-video.test.ts`; "ЧАМД ХАМААТАЙ ЮУ?" from Oct 31 2026, told like a news creator / journalist on Facebook since Nov 6 2026, the owner's ask): the most important published story of the last 36 h, else 4 days (importance ≥ `IMPACT_MIN_IMPORTANCE` 65, an AI summary that is not the fallback, not opendata.gov.mn, dated, no `impact:` or `money:` video yet, `videos.key` = `impact:<articleId>`). The voice opens "Сайн байна уу, Ил тод мэдээ." with the headline (white card + a "ГОЛ МЭДЭЭ / 30 секундэд" sticker), then "Юу болсон бэ?" (the summary's first one or two sentences, ≤ `WHAT_MAX` 210, word for word), then the grounded `reader_impact` cards that fit (ХЭНД only with ЮУ ӨӨРЧЛӨГДӨХ, ХЭЗЭЭНЭЭС, ЮУ ХИЙХ ВЭ) while the narration stays ≤ `IMPACT_MAX_CHARS` (480), and signs off "Эх сурвалж нь <source>. Энэ бол Ил тод мэдээ." (the source without "(gov.mn)"); the closing card reads "Баримтаар, эх сурвалжтай". A story needs a summary or a change with one more answer. Facts only: every card text is the stored `reader_title` / summary / `reader_impact` (`verifyImpactSpec` refuses any other), the frame says "Товчлол: AI (эх мэдээнээс) · Дуу: AI" and names the source, `weighsOrAccuses` headlines and any text naming a charge (`CHARGE`: хулгай, авлиг, шүүмж, хууль бус…) never become a video. The voice reads the texts after `spokenText` turns what a speech model misreads into words (ISO dates, "11-р сар" → "арван нэгдүгээр сар", "170.1 тэрбум" → "зуун дал гаруй тэрбум", separators, leading zeros, "УИХ-ын" → "Улсын Их Хурлын" with its case ending; plain numbers, percents, years and day endings ElevenLabs reads right, checked with a transcript Oct 31 2026). Post texts (`posts.ts`) list headline, what happened, who, change, when, action and the source, with no closing ask.
- The time comes from slots, not crons (`src/lib/video/schedule.ts` `VIDEO_SLOTS`: 12–16 and 19–23 Ulaanbaatar, each "30 СЕК МЭДЭЭ" first then prices (`kindsFor`); `dueSlot` = inside a slot whose start is after the newest video): GitHub started the first evening run 5.5 h late and a video went out at 01:12. Scheduled video.yml runs and the 30-minute pipeline both run `npm run video:make -- --slot`, so whichever comes first in the slot makes its one video; then `social:post --mode video` posts any video not yet posted (< 48 h). The pipeline steps are `continue-on-error`. Manual video.yml runs make one at once.

- No engagement asks (Nov 2026): Meta demotes posts that explicitly ask for comments, shares or tags, so videos end with a genuine question or a news sign-off only ("Танайд одоо хэд байна?", "Энэ бол Ил тод мэдээ."), the end-card stickers read "iltodmedee.mn" / "Эх сурвалжтай", and video post texts carry no "коммент бич / найздаа илгээ".
- Picture and sound (`scene-html.ts` + `timing.ts` + `render.ts`): 1080×1920 HTML in the poster look of `theme.ts`, the same as the social cards (Nov 2026, after the burgundy cinema look: the owner asked for the noorog.mn / mogul.plus style everywhere): red lit from above with a slow drifting light, strong film grain, a soft vignette, Oswald 700 capitals (`posterFontFace`, `oswald-700.woff`) in yellow `#ffd84a` and white, white cards with dark ink, yellow chips and labels, "logo | ИЛ ТОД МЭДЭЭ" with the series in yellow; each page appends `CINEMA_CSS` after its own rules (`!important` beats colours set inline per scene) and paints `cinemaBg(t)`), spring pop-ins, a progress bar, outlined word-by-word captions (3 words at a time, the spoken word lifted, `*…*` numbers in yellow, `captionWords`), all inside the Reels safe zone (nothing important above 200 px or below 1560 px); text reaches the page as escaped JSON and `textContent`. Playwright screenshots `render(t)` for every frame (30 fps) into ffmpeg (H.264 + AAC, faststart). `sentenceStarts` moves each sentence's letter-share estimate to the nearest `silencedetect` pause (≤ 1.2 s); `buildTimeline` shifts the starts by the guess pause and places the synthesized effects (`audioFilter`: tick ×3, pop, whoosh, bell, a click per block; no third-party audio) and a soft synthesized 100 BPM beat ducked under the voice (`sidechaincompress`; `VIDEO_MUSIC=off` drops it). ≈ 20 s, ≈ 2.5 MB. `CHROMIUM_PATH` points at a browser outside Playwright for local previews.
- Storage and serving: table `videos` (migration 0021, RLS, not in the backup) keeps the MP4 (≤ `MAX_BYTES` 4 MB, re-rendered at CRF 27 if bigger; Vercel answers ≤ 4.5 MB) for `VIDEO_KEEP_DAYS` (3), then `make-video` clears it; `/reel?v=<id>` (route handler, canonical query only, `video/mp4`, immutable CDN cache, 404 once cleared) is what Instagram (`media_type=REELS`, `share_to_feed`), Threads (`VIDEO` container, 36 status checks) and Facebook (`/{page}/video_reels` start → `rupload` with `file_url` → finish `PUBLISHED`, `publishReel`) fetch. `social_posts` kind `video`, ref = `videos.id`; only videos < 48 h old are posted. A video a platform clearly refused keeps its row as `post_id = 'failed:n'` and is tried at most `VIDEO_MAX_TRIES` (3) times in all (it used to be retried every 30 min for 48 h, leaving an unpublished upload on the Page each time); the third refusal is an `::error::`.
