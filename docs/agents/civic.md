# Civic data pages

Rules for this area, moved from `CLAUDE.md` with its section numbers. Keep each rule here, in one place; the reason behind a change goes to `docs/HISTORY.md`.

## 6h. Places ("Миний аймаг / дүүрэг")
- `src/lib/places.ts`: 21 aimags, the capital and its 9 districts, each with its scraped domains and its 2024 parliament.mn constituency string. Pages match MPs with `placeMatchesConstituency` (by the areas a constituency lists, not the exact string, so a map redrawn or reordered after an election still finds them; "аймаг"/"дүүрэг"/"N-р тойрог:" words ignored). "Сүхбаатар" is an aimag and a district: its "дүүрэг"/"аймаг" word or the other areas listed decide, alone and unmarked it matches neither. The capital page covers the city's sites plus every district's, and every district's (or a city-wide) constituency. The watchdog names places no active MP's constituency covers.
- `/place/<slug>` (prerendered for every place, `getPlaceFeed(domains)`: all published stories of those sites, no importance cut-off) shows the news, the constituency's MPs (links to /officials) and the official sites; `PlacePicker` (server markup, `#places`) is on every place page and on `/topic/local`; sitemap lists all places. ⭐ `FollowPlaceButton` on a place page stores its slug in localStorage `iltod-places` (`parsePlaceFollows`, `topic-follow.ts`); `/my` merges followed places (`/api/place-feed/<slug>`, prerendered per place, `getPlaceFeed(domains, 20)`) with followed topics. `tests/places.test.ts` pins the constituency strings and that every local source maps to a place.

## 6i. Promises ("Амлалт")
- `/promises` + `/promises/<n-m>` (16 prerendered direction pages): the Government's 2024–2028 action programme (УИХ-ын 2024 оны 21-р тогтоол, annex 1: 4 policies, 16 directions, 620 goals, 14 mega projects) verbatim, in its own numbering. `npm run promises:import` (`--dry-run`; `scripts/import-programme.ts`) downloads the official Word file from legalinfo.mn through `politeFetch`, reads it with `readZipEntry` (`src/lib/promises/zip.ts`, node:zlib only) and `parseProgramme` (`programme.ts`), and writes `src/lib/promises/programme-2024.json` (server-only, never sent to the client). Re-run only if the resolution is amended.
- Facts only: no status is computed and no AI judges a goal. "Тоон хэмжүүртэй" (`isMeasurable`) only says the goal's text names a number with a unit. The page lists the Prime Ministers since approval from `governments.ts` as labels (`governmentsBetween`, up to `GOVERNMENTS_VERIFIED_THROUGH`, with that date printed) and links the source. From `PROGRAMME_END` (2028-07-01, `programme.ts`) a daily cached clock (`programmeEnded`) makes the header say "өмнөх хөтөлбөр" instead of presenting it as current; the watchdog asks for `promises:import` then. `ProgrammeClock` (`programmeClock`, Nov 2026) shows the days left to `PROGRAMME_END` and the share of the programme's time passed, from a daily cached Ulaanbaatar clock, on /promises and each direction page, and says it is time, not goals met: 619 of the 620 goals name no date of their own (only 2.1.1.30, "2025 оныг Залуучуудын жил"), so per-goal deadlines would be invented. Implementation status comes later and only from official reports, with links. Annex 2 (indicators) is not in the Word file; find its official source before adding it.

## 6j. Projects ("Төслүүд")
- `/projects` + `/projects/<slug>` (prerendered per `PROJECTS`): the big announced projects (Шинэ Хархорум, Хүннү хот, the Алтанширээ refinery, Эгийн гол, Эрдэнэбүрэн, Тавантолгой 450 MW, Улаанбаатар метро, the national satellite, the Хөшигийн хөндий tunnel) as a curated dataset in `src/lib/projects/data.ts`: `announced` (the first official act with a source), `planned` cost (document + date), `budgets` (approved / amendment / plan / spent, ₮, each with its page), `events` (dated decisions and statements), `people` (official roles only, MP names link through `linkPeople`), `stage` (one of `STAGES`, read from the body's own statement: `basis` + `asOf` + source), `links` (official page, Шилэн данс org, `megaIndex` into `PROGRAMME.megaProjects`, search phrases). Dates are as precise as the source (`When`: "2018", "2025-05", "2025-03-26"; `whenLabel`, `whenToDate`). Facts only: official pages first, the press only where no official page states the fact, labelled by `sourceKind` ("албан" / "төрийн мэдээ" / "хэвлэл"); `officialSpent` sums only `spent` lines; `perPerson` divides by `POPULATION`; a fact whose page could not be read is left out, never guessed; `tests/projects.test.ts` refuses verdict words (үрсэн, алдсан, авлига…) in any text and dates after `verifiedThrough`. Pages are server markup (`StageLadder`, `ProjectRows`, `SourceLink` in `src/components/projects`); the detail page lists the budget table, timeline, people, links and the 5 newest archive stories (`search(phrase)` inside Suspense). The "N жил" clock is a daily `'use cache'` entry. `/promises#mega` links each programme mega project that has a page. The watchdog asks for a re-read 180 days after `PROJECTS_VERIFIED_THROUGH` (`HealthConfig.dated.projectsVerified`); when re-reading, move the date and update `verifiedThrough` per entry.

## 6g. Laws, attendance, weekly numbers
- `/laws` ("Хууль" in the nav, replacing "Сэдвүүд", which moved to the footer): `getLegalActs()` = legalinfo.mn articles with their AI summaries, grouped by day, each linking the original text.
- Attendance: the opendata.mn attendance highlight now keeps every member (not only the top 20; /data slices 20 itself). It is counted per member AND parliamentary term (`termOf` in `src/lib/officials/terms.ts`: terms start 1 July of years divisible by 4, no list to maintain), so a new member never inherits a former namesake's record and /data labels the term when the data spans two. `/officials/[id]` matches the member by `short_name` ("Х.Баттулга") within `termOf(term_start)` and shows present / travel / medical / leave / unexplained with the neutral note that "unexplained" is not proof of absence.
- `/digest` "📊 Энэ 7 хоногт": `getWeekNumbers()` plain counts (articles, sources, legal acts, articles naming ₮, high-risk), each tile linking to where they are.

## 6d. Officials & civic data (facts only)
- Rule: show official records with their source link; never let AI or copy accuse anyone. Computed markers are neutral ("анхаарах өөрчлөлт" with numbers, `src/lib/officials/flags.ts`).
- MP photos go through `MpPhoto` (`src/components/officials/mp-photo.tsx`): a parliament.mn `/uploads/` photo is served by next/image at its shown size (1x/2x; `images.remotePatterns` and `minimumCacheTTL` 30 days in next.config.ts), about 1.8 KB AVIF instead of a 47 KB JPEG; any other host stays a plain `<img>`. The member's own page loads it eagerly (`eager`, not the deprecated `priority`).
  - Fairness (`flags.ts`): a year whose total is `UNIT_GAP` (20×) away from the person's other years is a suspected unit error in the IAAC filing (thousand ₮ vs ₮; Д.Ганмаа 2025): `suspectUnitYears` names it (`unit_suspect` flag, every year when there are only two or the filings split evenly between two scales) and it is left out of % changes and markers; shown percentages are capped at `PCT_CAP` (500%+) in a neutral colour with the years compared; loans are flagged only above `LOANS_OVER_INCOME` (3×) the income; an empty filing is `total_assets = null`, never 0. A hidden year is never reported as a missing filing (`declarationFlags(rows, hiddenYears)`; a gap of several years lists each one). Editors can take a year out in /admin ("46:2025", `app_settings` key `officials_hidden_years`, `hidden.ts`, `saveHiddenYears` → `updateTag("officials")`); the profile says so and links the original filing.
  - Name collisions: two members can share a short name, at once (Д.Цогтбаатар ×2) or across terms (a new "Б.Бат" and a former one who may still be in the news as a minister). `attributable(people)` (pass every politician, inactive included) = active members whose short name no other member, current or former, shares: article people links (`linkPeople` with `getPoliticianNames()`), follow alerts (`mentionAlerts`, `send-push` reads all members) and the weekly "most named" (`post-social` reads all members) use it. The profile refuses news mentions when the name was ever shared (`nameShared`) and attendance only when it is shared now (`nameSharedNow`; attendance is per term). Declaration gaps (`missing_year`) count only years inside the member's seat (`declarationFlags(rows, hidden, term)`).
- `npm run officials:sync` (`--dry-run`, `--limit n`): parliament.mn `api/parliament_members_list/` + `api/parliament_member/detail/{id}/` (its `hom_url` links the ХАСХОМ id) → `politicians`; IAAC `xacxom.iaac.mn/api/public/xacxom/{id}` (last 5 years, thousand ₮ → ₮) → `asset_declarations`. Name search (`/api/public/xacxom/search`) only when `hom_url` is missing and the name is unique. Weekly workflow `officials-sync.yml`. Members missing from a full roster read become `is_active=false` (kept), so elections need no code change. A roster is "full" only with ≥ 100 members and ≥ 80 % of the members active now (`mayRetire`, `roster-guard.ts`); otherwise nobody is retired and the step is red. parliament.mn shape changes read as unknown, not empty (`termKnown`, `electionKnown` from `.nullish().catch(null)` schemas): `store()` then keeps the stored term / constituency / votes instead of writing null, and > 50 % unknown turns the step red (`detailsDegraded`).
- "Мэдүүлсэн компаниуд" on `/officials/[id]` (Oct 2026): the organisations a member names in their own latest ХАСХОМ filing (`details.stocks`, `details.lenders`; `declaredCompanies` in `src/lib/officials/companies.ts`: one entry per `orgKey`, so "Бодит чадал ХХК" = "Бодитчадал ХХК"; names that are not organisations (`isOrgName`) are private people: counted, never shown or looked up; banks, funds and Эрдэнэс Тавантолгой are listed but not looked up). `npm run officials:companies` (`--budget` min, `--limit`, `--dry-run --names "A ХХК,B ХХК"` without a DB; weekly step in `officials-sync.yml`, 25 min) searches each name in Шилэн данс (`getBeltgenNiiluulegch?orgName=`, as written then without spaces, every page of hits up to `MAX_SEARCH_PAGES`; a longer list throws and the previous result stays) and links it only when exactly one register has that exact key (`exactMatches`; two or more = "ambiguous", never linked), then reads the paying state bodies (`getSupplierOne?registerNo=`, top 3) from `PAYMENTS_FROM` (2017-01-01). Results live in `indicator_snapshots` key `officials_companies` (~100 KB for ~340 names, watchdog 14 d), rechecked after `RECHECK_DAYS` (30), never-checked names first; the network half is `companies-sync.ts` so pages never load the scraper. The page says a payment record is not wrongdoing and how the match is made. Tender wins (Nov 2026, `getCompanyTenders`, `winnerKeys` / `matchWinners` / `foundKeys`): a declared company Шилэн данс knows under exactly one register (`found`) whose key appears in a form-1831 winner cell (each company of a consortium cell counts) gets "Тендерт шалгарсан: N гэрээ" under its payments line, and /tenders `#mp-companies` lists the lots with the members who declared shares in it (latest filing, active members), saying a match is not wrongdoing; both appear only when there is a match (none on Nov 6 2026: 33 found companies, ~8 000 lots). Which winner cells name which company is worked out at each `tenders:sync` and `officials:companies` run (`storeCompanyMatches`, `src/lib/procurement/company-matches.ts`) and stored in `indicator_snapshots` key `tender_company_matches`; `getCompanyTenders` reads that row (the matches and, for each matched company, the members who declared it) and never matches live: until a sync (or the demo seed) stores the row, no member shows tender wins (Nov 10 2026: the live fallback ran on every member page of every deploy while the row was missing, ~135 MB of egress in a day). On Vercel `use cache` does not outlive a serverless instance, so the live match read all ~3 700 winner names (~170 KB) on every `/officials/[id]` and `/tenders` render, the database's largest reader on Nov 9 2026. Registry owners/directors (opendata.burtgel.gov.mn: robots.txt `Disallow: /`) and spouses are not available from an official open source: never infer family links.
- "Миний гишүүн": `/officials` has an aimag/district picker (the `PLACES` that elect a member, matched with `placeMatchesConstituency`, so the aimag and the district Сүхбаатар are two options) and ⭐ follow buttons (also on `/officials/[id]`).
